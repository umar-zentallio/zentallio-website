  1. 01
    Orders · One queue

    Every channel, one queue.

    Store, web, app, Amazon, eBay and Etsy orders all land in one queue. #803120 — an Aria Wool Coat in M, $299 on Amazon — is captured and routed to ship-from-store, one of 14 re-routed in the last hour.

  2. 02
    Inventory · By location

    Live stock, every door.

    On-hand, inbound and size health for every store and DC. Lyon's M is out and Marseille is under two weeks' cover — so Iris raises an 18-unit transfer from the Roissy DC before either runs dry.

  3. 03
    Allocation · Channel pool

    One pool, no oversell.

    78 coats split across every live listing, each with a safety reserve. eBay's buffer is about to break, so Iris moves 6 units across from the Web pool — and keeps Etsy unlisted for this style.

  4. 04
    Pricing · Price book

    Fees in, margin held.

    A price for every channel, with marketplace fees folded in. Amazon's 15% fee would crush the coat's margin, so Iris lifts its Amazon list to $299 to protect the net-margin floor.

  5. 05
    Dispatch · Every carrier

    Shipped against the promise.

    #803120 leaves the Paris store with Chronopost, due today by 18:00. When Nice slips to SLA risk, Iris re-routes the next 5 outerwear orders to the Roissy DC to keep the promise date.

  6. 06
    Returns · RMA desk

    Every return has a reason.

    Every RMA is tracked from reason to restock. Size & fit drives 46% of returns, clustered on two slim styles — Iris flags the size spec and trims the tail-size re-buy before the next order ships.

  7. 07
    Reviews · Feedback

    Reviews that reach the product.

    Ratings and sentiment flow back to the style. The Halden Slim Jean's 2-star reviews centre on fit and its returns are up 3pt — Iris ties it to the size spec and routes it to merchandising.

  8. 08
    Iris · The call

    Iris makes the call.

    A size break forms in Women's Outerwear — core M/L selling out, XS/XXL overstocked. Iris prices it: a size-targeted markdown holds 1.7pt of margin over a blanket cut, and full-price sales stay protected.
