About Numerus

Numerus is the agentic finance platform from Zentallio — the working surface a CFO operates from end-to-end: statements, ledgers, bank, reconciliation, inventory, tax, retail and analytics, each with the agent's analysis layered on top. This is v1.9.3, configured for the Tavolo Foods pilot.

Trial Balance now shows real data — Tavolo Foods FY 2025-26, 287 GL accounts from the uploaded TB, classified and summed by class. Everything else (P&L, BS, CF, AR, AP, cash, etc.) is still illustrative Tavolo Foods mock — internally consistent, but not yet wired to the same source. The TB screen demonstrates Numerus rendering real ERPNext-shape data at scale.

Two layers of intelligence run in every module. The AI agent — the workbench — reasons, drafts and acts, doing the routine 80%. The ML models — the BI — predict and detect: forecasting cash and demand, scoring risk, flagging anomalies. The models surface a signal; the agent reasons over it and recommends. You stay in control.

The agent's output shown throughout — commentary, flags, variance analysis, recommendations, drafted messages — represents the orchestrator. Live backend wiring lands in v0.2.
