Iris · L1 · Zen Rules 02 / 03

Appointment & consultation studio

A bridal consultation is not a table for two — it is a room, a stylist, a rail of samples and ninety uninterrupted minutes. The book has to reserve all four or it has reserved nothing.

01

An appointment reserves room, stylist and sample rail together, so a double-booked fitting room cannot happen.

02

Fitting slots are held against the production stage that needs them, which is why a fitting slot appears as a slip cause on the risk board.

03

The client's file, past orders and stated occasion are surfaced before the consultation begins, not looked up during it.

04

Party size is captured, because a bride arriving with six is a different room and a different hour from a bride arriving alone.

05

No-shows and rescheduling are tracked against the promise date, since a missed fitting moves a delivery date whether anyone says so or not.

Iris · L1 · Zen Rules 03 / 03

Deposit, instalment & cancellation control

1.1M is held against garments not yet delivered. Every rupee of it is somebody's deposit on a date — and the rules governing it should not be a conversation at the counter.

01

Deposit schedules are configured per order type, and the balance due at each stage is a rule rather than a negotiation.

02

Instalments are tracked against production stage, so money and progress stay in step across a six-month order.

03

Cancellation terms apply by rule against how far the garment has advanced — cut cloth cannot be refunded like an untouched order.

04

Deposit exposure is reported live as the liability it is, not as revenue already earned.

05

Overdue instalments surface before the next stage starts, which is the only moment holding work is still a fair option.

Iris · L2 · Zen Models 02 / 03

Atelier & karigar capacity planning

Capacity as a number rather than a hope. Work loaded by skill and stage — embellishment, cutting, finishing — so an over-commitment is visible weeks before it becomes a phone call.

01

Load is modelled per stage and per skill, at 89.6% accuracy, because an atelier's real constraint is rarely the whole workshop.

02

Every order consumes hours from the specific stages it passes through, so committing an order is committing a calendar.

03

A new order cannot be dated against a stage that is already full — the promise is checked before it is made, not after.

04

Contract karigar capacity is modelled as buyable overflow, with the cost of buying it shown against the cost of the slip.

05

Seasonal peaks — wedding season, Eid, festival drops — are forecast against the same stage model rather than absorbed by overtime.

Iris · L2 · Zen Models 03 / 03

Unstitched & lawn drop planning

The other half of the business. A lawn drop is a volume event with one shot at it — sized per city, released in hours, and either right or discounted for the rest of the season.

01

Run size is modelled per city at 90.7% accuracy, because Lahore, Karachi and Dubai do not want the same volume of the same design.

02

Design-level demand is predicted before print, which is the only point at which the quantity can still be changed.

03

The drop is released across counter, web and stockists on one live stock pool rather than three allocations that drift.

04

Sell-through in the first hours is read against the projection, so a restock decision is made while the print run is still viable.

05

Volume and bespoke never compete for the same atelier hours — they are planned as two businesses that share a building.
