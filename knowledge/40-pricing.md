# Pricing (policy + model for Iris)

Source: Zentallio Price Configuration Workbook (Sector Edition). Figures are
**illustrative baselines** — Iris may share them as ballpark ranges to help a
visitor, but must always (a) frame them as illustrative, (b) note the real price
depends on region, number of locations and solution mix, and (c) offer a call
with a consultant for a firm, validated quote. Iris must not present any number
as a committed/final price, and must not invent figures beyond this file.

## How Zentallio pricing works
- **Per-location monthly subscription**, modular — start with one solution or run
  the whole platform.
- The platform is **6 core solutions + Iris**: Balanced Scorecard, Point of Sale,
  Numerus (CFO), Nexus (supply chain), Motus (operations), Manus (workforce), and
  **Iris** as one flat AI-layer plan (not tiered by level).
- **Sector bundles** — bundling the full set is cheaper than buying à la carte.
- **Volume discounts** grow with locations; **annual prepay** is cheaper than
  monthly; a **bundle discount** applies to the full set.
- **Regional pricing** — home-market baseline, higher for US (pilot / standard).
- **No hardware lock-in, no long-term contract, cancel anytime after the pilot.**
- **Payments & hardware are separate pass-through** — Zentallio processes payments
  via **Stripe Connect**; Stripe's fees and any Stripe Terminal hardware are billed
  separately and are **not** part of the software subscription.

## Illustrative pricing (baseline = home-market / Pakistan USD, per location per month)
Always present these as *illustrative* and route to a call for a real quote.

- **Per solution (starting points):** Balanced Scorecard ~$50 · Point of Sale ~$80 ·
  Numerus ~$70 · Nexus ~$55 · Motus ~$45 · Manus ~$40 · Iris ~$75.
- **Whole platform:** ~$415/location/month à la carte, ~**$374/location/month
  bundled** (about a 10% bundle discount) at the home-market baseline.
- **Discounts:** bundle ~10%; annual prepay ~8%; volume roughly 5% (10+ locations),
  10% (50+), 15% (100+), up to ~20% (250+).
- **Region:** the above is the home-market baseline; **US pricing is higher**
  (roughly 2.5× for early pilots, ~3.5× standard). Give a firm regional number via
  a consultant rather than computing it live.

## Payments & hardware (Stripe — separate, pass-through, not a Zentallio software fee)
- **Processing:** in-person ~2.7% + $0.05 · online ~2.9% + $0.30 · manually keyed
  +0.5% · international card +1.5% · currency conversion +1.0% · ACH ~0.8% (capped $5).
- **Terminal hardware (one-time):** Stripe Reader M2 ~$59 · BBPOS WisePOS E ~$249 ·
  Stripe Reader S700 ~$499 · Tap to Pay on iPhone $0 (uses an existing phone).
- These are billed by Stripe / pass through to the client; they are not marked into
  the software subscription.

## How Iris should answer "how much?"
Give the model + an illustrative range, then offer a real quote:
"As an illustrative baseline the full platform runs about $374 per location per
month bundled (individual solutions start lower — Iris and the CFO module in the
$70s, POS around $80), before regional pricing and volume discounts. Your actual
number depends on which solutions you run, how many locations, and your region —
shall I set up a quick call for a firm quote?" Then capture their details / book.
Payments and hardware are separate (Stripe pass-through) — mention if relevant.
