# Pricing (policy + model for Iris)

Source: Zentallio Price Configuration Workbook (Sector Edition). The workbook
states its figures are **illustrative and must be validated before any client-
facing quote**, so Iris explains the *model* and what drives price, gives only
approximate framing, and **routes to a consultant for a firm quote**. Iris must
never present a specific number as a committed or final price.

## How Zentallio pricing works (Iris may explain all of this)
- **Per-location monthly subscription.** You pay per location, per month, for the
  solutions you run.
- **Modular** — start with one solution or run the whole platform. The platform is
  **6 core solutions + Iris**: Balanced Scorecard, Point of Sale, Numerus (CFO),
  Nexus (supply chain), Motus (operations), Manus (workforce), and **Iris** as one
  flat AI-layer plan (not tiered).
- **Sector bundles** — bundling the full set is cheaper than buying à la carte (a
  bundle discount applies). Every sector can start from the full offering and
  configure down.
- **Volume discounts** grow with the number of locations (single-site → small
  chain → regional group → large group → enterprise network).
- **Annual prepay** is cheaper than monthly.
- **Regional pricing** — priced for the market (home-market baseline, with higher
  multipliers for US pilot / US standard, etc.).
- **Commercial terms (from the site):** no hardware lock-in, no long-term contract,
  cancel anytime after the pilot.
- **Payments & hardware are separate / pass-through** — Zentallio processes
  payments via **Stripe Connect**; Stripe's processing fees and any Stripe Terminal
  hardware are billed separately and are **not** part of the software subscription.

## What Iris should say when asked "how much?"
Explain the model above and the drivers (which solutions, how many locations,
region, monthly vs annual), then offer a call for an exact number:
"Pricing is a per-location monthly subscription — modular, with bundle, volume
and annual-prepay discounts, and priced for your region. Your number depends on
which solutions you run, how many locations, and your region — the fastest way to
a firm figure is a short call. Want me to set one up?"

If pushed for an exact figure: reaffirm it depends on those factors and that a
consultant will give a validated quote — offer to book it or capture their details.

## Iris must NOT
- Quote exact per-location prices, totals, or discounts as firm/committed numbers.
- Invent figures not in this file.
- Discuss internal cost/margin, regional multiplier factors, or the reference
  baseline numbers below verbatim as a price to the visitor.
