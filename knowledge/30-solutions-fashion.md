## By sector

### Apparel & Ready-to-Wear
High volume, wide size curves, short seasons. Margin is won or lost on how fast you read a size break — and how late you can hold a markdown.

Formats: Men's Ready-to-Wear, Women's Ready-to-Wear, Denim & Casual, Fast-Fashion Chain, Workwear & Uniform

How key modules apply here:
- **scorecard** — Sell-through by style, size-curve health and weeks-of-cover — every door on one board.
- **pos** — Size and colour matrix at the till, barcode or RFID, exchange and store credit without leaving the sale.
- **oms** — One stock pool across web and floor — ship-from-store, click & collect, endless aisle.
- **numerus** — Style-level gross margin after markdown, freight and returns.

### Ethnic, Bridal & Couture
One customer, one measurement file, one delivery date that cannot move. Value sits in the order book, not on the shelf.

Formats: Ethnic Pret, Unstitched & Lawn, Bridal Couture, Luxury Formal, Designer Atelier, Appointment Studio, Festive & Occasion Edit

How key modules apply here:
- **scorecard** — Order-book value, atelier load, promise-date risk and deposit exposure in one view.
- **pos** — Deposit and instalment capture, measurement profile attached to the customer, alteration ticket raised at the counter.
- **oms** — The made-to-order pipeline end to end — booking, fitting, alteration, final handover.
- **numerus** — Job-level costing: fabric, karigar, embellishment and alteration against the quoted price.

### Kidswear & Teen
The customer grows out of you every season. Age-band demand, school calendars and gifting spikes shape the whole year.

Formats: Infant & Newborn, Kids 2–12, Teen & Tween, Maternity & Nursing, School Uniform

How key modules apply here:
- **scorecard** — Age-band sell-through, school-season readiness and repeat-family rate.
- **pos** — Gift receipts, sibling bundles and school-list checkout that scans the whole list in one go.
- **oms** — School-list pre-order with collection windows, so intake week doesn't become a queue.
- **numerus** — Season-block margin — back-to-school, Eid, winter — costed separately, not averaged.

### Sportswear & Activewear
Technical stock, brand-partner terms and drop culture. Sizing is deep, sell-out is the goal, and the launch window is short.

Formats: Performance Sportswear, Athleisure & Studio, Outdoor & Adventure, Team & Club Kit

How key modules apply here:
- **scorecard** — Drop sell-out rate, brand-partner sell-through and full-price ratio.
- **pos** — Limited-release checkout with serialised items, member pricing and club orders taken at the counter.
- **oms** — Launch queue, raffle allocation and reserve-and-collect, without the site falling over.
- **numerus** — Brand-partner margin after rebate, co-op marketing and returns.

### Footwear
Depth beats breadth. A size run with a hole in it is a lost sale you never see, and nobody on the floor reports it.

Formats: Sneaker & Streetwear, Formal & Dress Shoes, Sandals & Khussa, Boots & Outdoor, Kids Footwear, Sports & Running

How key modules apply here:
- **scorecard** — Size-run completeness, pairs per hour and full-price sell-through by door.
- **pos** — Barcode or box-label scan, try-on capture, exchange and store credit without leaving the sale.
- **oms** — Single-pair transfers between doors, endless aisle, and reserve-and-collect on a size you don't hold.
- **numerus** — Category margin after markdown, freight and the returns that footwear generates more of than anything else.

### Cosmetics & Beauty
Dated stock, hundreds of shades, and a tester budget nobody costs. Beauty is an expiry problem wearing a merchandising problem.

Formats: Colour Cosmetics, Skincare, Fragrance, Haircare, Beauty Counter & Concession, Tester & Sampling Bar

How key modules apply here:
- **scorecard** — Repeat-purchase rate, shade availability, expiry exposure and counter productivity on one board.
- **pos** — Batch and expiry captured at the till, counter and BA attribution, sampling logged against the sale.
- **oms** — Replenishment and refill orders, click & collect, and transfers routed to clear short-dated stock first.
- **numerus** — Margin after tester consumption, expiry write-off, BA commission and concession settlement.

### Fabric & Textile
Sold by the metre, bought by the roll, and cut with a wastage nobody logs. The yield is the business.

Formats: Fabric & Yardage House, Unstitched Textile Retail, Suiting & Shirting, Occasion & Prayer Textiles, Mill Outlet

How key modules apply here:
- **scorecard** — Metre-level sell-through, cut wastage and roll ageing — by branch and by cutting table.
- **pos** — Cut-length pricing, roll and lot tracking, and remnant handling done at the counter, not in a notebook.
- **oms** — Bulk and cut-length orders, roll reservations, and branch transfers that keep lot integrity intact.
- **numerus** — Yield and wastage costed per roll and per lot, so gross margin means something.

### Home Textiles & Furnishing
Sold as a piece, bought as a room. A duvet without its matching sheet is half a sale — and a curtain order lives or dies on the fitting date.

Formats: Home Linen & Bedding, Curtains & Upholstery, Towels & Bath, Rugs & Floor Coverings, Table & Kitchen Linen, Cushions & Soft Furnishing

How key modules apply here:
- **scorecard** — Made-to-measure pipeline health, on-time installation, set attach rate and room-set sell-through.
- **pos** — Per-piece and cut-length pricing, deposit capture, measurement notes and the install date booked at the counter.
- **oms** — Measure, make, deliver and install as one order — with the promise date visible to the customer and the fitter.
- **numerus** — Job-level costing for made-to-measure — fabric, making, fitting visits and remakes — against the quoted price.

### Marketplace & Online Selling
Every channel takes a different cut and promises a different date. The margin is real only after the fees, the returns and the ads.

Formats: Own D2C Store, Global Marketplaces, Social Commerce, Quick-commerce & Aggregators, Resale & Pre-owned

How key modules apply here:
- **scorecard** — Channel margin after fees, order accuracy, return rate and SLA performance side by side.
- **pos** — In-store returns for online orders, and store-fulfilled marketplace orders raised at the same counter.
- **oms** — The core of this sector — one stock pool, order routing by SLA, returns anywhere, oversell prevented.
- **numerus** — Commission, shipping, returns and ad spend charged to the order that caused them.
