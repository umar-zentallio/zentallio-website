# Sectors Zentallio serves

Two industries, one platform and one AI (Iris).

- **Quick Service & Street Food** — e.g. Burger joint, Pizza (delivery/dine-in), Shawarma & wraps, Fried chicken, Sandwiches & subs, Tacos & burritos
- **Casual Dining** — e.g. Pakistani/desi restaurant, Chinese restaurant, BBQ & grill house, Seafood restaurant, Italian (sit-down), Lebanese & Middle Eastern
- **Fine Dining & Premium** — e.g. Continental fine dine, Fusion fine dine, Steakhouse, Chef’s table / tasting menu, Rooftop restaurant
- **Café, Coffee & Bakery** — e.g. Specialty coffee shop, All-day café, Bakery café, Dessert café, Co-working café, Artisan bakery
- **Ice Cream, Desserts & Sweets** — e.g. Ice cream parlour, Gelato shop, Waffle & crepe house, Shakes & dessert bar, Desi mithai (halwa/kheer), Candy & confectionery
- **Beverages & Drinks** — e.g. Juice bar & smoothie bar, Bubble tea / boba shop, Fresh sugarcane & nimbu pani, Mocktail & shisha lounge, Bar & pub, Brew café
- **Health, Wellness & Specialty Diets** — e.g. Salad bar, Vegan / plant-based, Organic & farm-to-table, Protein & fitness meal prep, Gluten-free specialist
- **Cloud Kitchen & Delivery-Only** — e.g. Virtual restaurant brand, Multi-brand cloud kitchen, Meal kit delivery, Dark store (grocery + F&B)
- **Institutional & B2B Food Service** — e.g. Corporate cafeteria, Hospital canteen, School / university canteen, Catering company, Food truck, Airline / travel catering
- **Niche & Experience Concepts** — e.g. Breakfast & brunch-only, Late night / 24-hour eatery, Pop-up & supper club, Food hall / market stall, Themed restaurant, Ghost restaurant
