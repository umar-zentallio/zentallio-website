Motus — Supply Chain & Inventory Movement

Pitch deck · Product overview · 8 min read

The problem

Between a supplier's truck and a finished plate, inventory moves through receiving, storage, prep, and waste — and at every one of those steps, a small, unmeasured loss can hide. A yield that's slightly off from a supplier substitution. A portion that's slightly generous during a rush. A cold-chain break that spoils product before anyone notices the temperature log. None of these show up as a single dramatic event. They show up, weeks later, as a food-cost variance with no obvious single cause — because the cause was never one event, it was a pattern across inventory movement that nobody was tracking end to end.

The product

Motus tracks inventory from the moment it's received to the moment it's sold or wasted — giving operators the movement-level visibility that a monthly count and a COGS percentage can't provide on their own.

Key features

Receiving to plate, tracked continuously

  • Every receipt, transfer, prep conversion, and waste event logged against the item it affects
  • Supplier yield tracked per delivery, not assumed to match the spec sheet
  • Portion-level usage cross-referenced against POS sales to catch drift as it happens, not at the next physical count

Cold-chain and quality monitoring

  • IoT temperature sensor integration for refrigeration and cold storage
  • Automatic alerts on excursions, with the duration and likely affected inventory flagged immediately
  • Full compliance log for health inspections and internal audits

Procurement intelligence

  • Supplier pricing and yield history available at the point of a purchasing decision, not buried in old invoices
  • Volume rebate and contract term tracking, so a promised discount doesn't get missed at reconciliation
  • Multi-supplier comparison for the same SKU across regions and time

Waste, made visible instead of assumed

  • Waste logged by cause — spoilage, prep error, over-production, customer return — instead of one undifferentiated line
  • Store and item-group level waste patterns surfaced automatically, not discovered by a manager who happens to notice
  • Ties directly into the same portion-drift detection that flags food-cost variance elsewhere in the platform

Integrates with

  • POS — sales data reconciles against inventory movement automatically
  • Numerus — COGS ledger entries stay tied to actual physical movement, not a periodic estimate
  • Manus — staffing gaps get checked against the waste and yield patterns that tend to follow them
  • Nexus — supplier and delivery data joins the same timeline as everything else

Why Zentallio built this

A monthly inventory count can tell you that something went missing. It can't tell you when, where in the process, or why. Motus exists to close that gap — so the answer to "why did food cost move" is traceable to an actual event, not a shrug and a re-order for next month.

See it on your data

Motus is built to work with your existing suppliers and receiving process — no requirement to change vendors to get movement-level visibility.
