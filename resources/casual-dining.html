Split Bill — By Item or Equally

A table's bill can be split any way the guests choose — equally per head, by individual items ordered, or a custom combination. No mental arithmetic for staff.

01

Equal split — total divided by number of guests, each paying their share by card or cash.

02

Item-by-item — each guest selects their own items and pays exactly what they ordered.

03

Multi-tender per split — Guest A pays cash, Guest B taps card, on the same bill.

04

Service charge distributed proportionally across splits — no manual calculation.
