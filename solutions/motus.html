How it decides

How Motus decides.

Iris is the AI that narrates every screen, flags what needs a human and acts on the rest once approved. It names the cause, costs the move and proposes the action.

L1

Zen Rules

Deterministic thresholds that fire on the shift the breach happens — no model lag, no waiting for a report.

L2

Zen Models

Forecasting and ML models that predict early enough to act on, with the cause named and the move costed.

L3

Ask Iris

Conversational layer: you ask what moved and why, and Iris answers with the recommended action for a human to approve.

Iris recommends; a human approves. Prices, stock, staff and money never move without approval.
